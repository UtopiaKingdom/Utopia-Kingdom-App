        Stay safe

        Quick heads up

        A lot of “easy money” online is just fraud

        Telegram VIP rooms, affiliate pyramids, home mining kits, dead wallet finders. Same story every time: big promises, then fees, malware, or your funds gone. This is a short cheat sheet so you spot it early.

        Crypto mining

        Home mining almost never profits

        Cheap “plug in and earn” boxes and cloud miner dashboards are classic bait. Residential power is expensive, and retail GPUs lose to real farms. If the pitch is passive income with zero work, walk away.

        $0.16 to $0.26

        Typical EU home kWh. Already too expensive for thin mining margins.

        Under $0.08

        Ballpark power where mining even starts to pencil out

        Without industrial hardware and cheap power, “mining income” is usually just your electricity bill with extra fans.

        Wallet finders

        “Abandoned wallet” tools are almost always malware

        Private keys are astronomically large. Apps that claim to hunt funded dead wallets are fake dashboards or stealers. They want your seed, clipboard, or wallet files, not to hand you free coins.

        ~0%

        Practical hit rate on correctly generated keys

        High

        Risk of seed theft, clipboard stealers, remote access

        If a key isn’t yours, taking the coins isn’t clever. It’s theft. And the “finder” usually never finds anything for you.

        Telegram and affiliates

        VIP groups, signal rooms, deposit unlocks

        • Pay to join, then “gurus” who only win in screenshots
        • Affiliate pyramids: you earn only if you recruit, not from trading skill
        • “Cloud miner” balances that climb until withdrawal needs a tax or fee
        • Support that asks for seed phrases, passwords, SSID dumps, or remote desktop

        If payout means sending more crypto first, close the chat and leave.

        Red flags

        Protect yourself with a few simple rules

        • Nobody needs your seed phrase, 2FA codes, or remote desktop to “help”
        • Guaranteed profits, locked withdrawals, and “upgrade to unlock” fees are traps
        • If income depends on recruiting friends, it is a pyramid, not a product
        • Slow down. Real platforms never rush you into depositing more to get your money out

        When something smells off, trust that feeling. Fraud thrives on urgency and FOMO.
