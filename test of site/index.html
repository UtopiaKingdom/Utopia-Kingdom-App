LUMIX

Fast scalping bridge using 1‑minute candles; emits short signals with a typical 5‑minute expiry and can route signals to the extension for one‑click or automated execution.

MIRAX

Heuristic market scanner that evaluates setups and applies quality/payout gates. MIRAX issues short 1‑minute signals (≈60s expiry) and schedules retries/locks when configured.

ARVION

Longer‑horizon strategy focused on 15‑minute expiry signals. ARVION watches multi‑frame candles (1m/5m/15m), manages trade lifecycle, and integrates with the WebSocket/extension.
